CLIPSMITH - FREE, PRIVATE, ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
===============================================
Nothing is put online. No keys or payment needed.


1) INSTALL NODE.JS (one time)
-----------------------------
Go to https://nodejs.org and click the green "Windows Installer (.msi)" button
(scroll down below the black code box). Open it and click Next through everything.
If a black window asks to install "Additional Tools" (Chocolatey/Python), just close it.
Restart your computer afterwards.


2) START CLIPSMITH
------------------
Windows: double-click  start-windows.bat
         (if "Windows protected your PC" appears: click More info > Run anyway)
Mac:     open Terminal, type  cd  and a space, drag this folder into the window,
         press Enter, then type  bash start-mac.sh  and press Enter.

The first start installs what it needs (a few minutes). Then your browser opens.
If it doesn't, open Chrome or Edge and go to:  http://localhost:4321/studio
Keep the black window open while you use the app. Close it to stop.


3) MAKE CLIPS (all free)
------------------------
1. Drop in a video. For your first try, use a short one (5-10 minutes).
2. Choose the language spoken in the video.
3. Choose how to pick clips:
   - "With Claude chat" (best): the app makes your captions, then gives you a
     prompt. Click "Copy prompt", open a new chat at claude.ai, paste it (Ctrl+V),
     send, copy Claude's whole answer, paste it back into the app, click
     "Create my clips".
   - "Laugh detector": fully automatic. Finds where people burst out laughing.
     Works best on podcasts/streams where people laugh out loud.
4. Click "Get clips in 1 click". Keep the tab open.
   The FIRST time, the free speech model downloads (about 80-250 MB, once).
   Free captions run on your own computer, so they take a while: roughly as long
   as the video itself, often faster on a newer PC.
5. Click a clip to edit: trim it, fix any wrong caption word (double-click it in
   the transcript), change caption style, framing, colours.
6. Export tab > "Export MP4". "Export all" on the clip grid downloads every clip.
7. Optional: Brand kit (left menu) to add your logo to every clip.


TIPS
----
- Use Chrome or Edge.
- "Caption settings" on the home screen: "More accurate (slower)" gives better
  captions for hard-to-hear videos.
- Projects are saved in your browser. To keep editing one later, open it and
  select the same video file again when asked.
- Optional paid speed-ups (not needed): double-click add-paid-keys.bat to add
  OpenAI / Anthropic keys, then choose "OpenAI (paid, faster)" captions or
  "Claude API" clip picking.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "Free captioning failed: Failed to fetch": you need internet the first time,
  so the speech model can download. Check your connection and try again.
- 'npm' is not recognized: Node.js isn't installed yet; install it and restart.
- No clips from the laugh detector: the video has no loud laughs; use
  "With Claude chat" instead.
- The page is blank or stuck: refresh it.
Send a screenshot of any error and it can be fixed.

Only clip videos you own or have permission to use.
