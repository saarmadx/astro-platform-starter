CLIPSMITH - FREE, PRIVATE, ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
===============================================
Nothing is put online. No keys or payment needed.


1) INSTALL NODE.JS (one time)
-----------------------------
Go to https://nodejs.org and click the green "Windows Installer (.msi)" button
(scroll down below the black code box). Open it and click Next through everything.
If a black window asks to install "Additional Tools" (Chocolatey/Python), just close it.
Restart your computer afterwards.


2) START CLIPSMITH
------------------
Windows: double-click  start-windows.bat
         (if "Windows protected your PC" appears: click More info > Run anyway)
Mac:     open Terminal, type  cd  and a space, drag this folder into the window,
         press Enter, then type  bash start-mac.sh  and press Enter.

The first start installs what it needs (a few minutes). Then your browser opens.
If it doesn't, open Chrome or Edge and go to:  http://localhost:4321/studio
Keep the black window open while you use the app. Close it to stop.


3) MAKE CLIPS (all free)
------------------------
1. Home: choose "Find clips" (or "Captions only" to caption a whole video).
   Drop in one video, or SEVERAL at once (they are done one after another).
2. Pick a TEMPLATE (e.g. "Streamer chaos") so every clip is styled for you.
3. Choose how to pick clips:
   - "With Claude chat" (smartest): copy the prompt into claude.ai, paste the
     answer back, click "Create my clips".
   - "Laugh detector" (automatic): scores every second for laughs, chat
     explosions, hype/rage in your voice and words like "NO WAY", "bro what".
   For streams choose "Gaming" under "What are you looking for?" and type the
   game (e.g. GTA 6) in "Game or topic" - it's added as a hashtag.
4. Click the big button. Keep the tab open. The first time, the free speech
   model downloads once.
5. Click a clip to open the editor. TIMELINE shortcuts (like Premiere Pro):
     Space play/pause      J K L back/pause/play    Left/Right one frame
     C razor (click to cut)   Ctrl+K split at the playhead   V normal pointer
     Q cut from part start to playhead     W cut from playhead to part end
     Delete remove the selected part (gap closes)    Ctrl+Z undo
     M marker   T add text   Z zoom   S snapping on/off   Up/Down jump to cuts
     "+5s before / +5s after" grabs more video if a clip is too tight.
   Click a part of the video (V1 row) to give it its own layout:
   Facecam + gameplay, facecam bubble (PiP), full screen, or fit + blur.
   Right-click a part for the same options.
6. Editor tabs: Captions (18 styles, fix words, translate), Layout, Effects
   (hook title, zooms, transitions at cuts, sounds, replay, shake, freeze,
   text, music), Post (titles, description, hashtags), Thumbnail, Export.
7. Brand kit (left menu): your NAME/@handle on every clip (moving anti-theft
   option) and your logo.
8. Post planner (left menu): plan when each clip goes up, copy the upload text,
   checklist, reminders, find your best posting times, how-to guides.


TIPS
----
- Use Chrome or Edge.
- "Caption settings" on the home screen: "Best" (shown when your graphics card
  can be used) gives the most accurate captions; it downloads about 1 GB once.
  Without a usable graphics card, "Accurate" is the most accurate choice.
- Exports now use several processor cores at once, so they are much faster.
- Projects are saved in your browser. To keep editing one later, open it and
  select the same video file again when asked.
- Optional paid speed-ups (not needed): double-click add-paid-keys.bat to add
  OpenAI / Anthropic keys, then choose "OpenAI (paid, faster)" captions or
  "Claude API" clip picking.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "Free captioning failed: Failed to fetch": you need internet the first time,
  so the speech model can download. Check your connection and try again.
- 'npm' is not recognized: Node.js isn't installed yet; install it and restart.
- Updating: unzip the new version over the old folder or into a new one, then
  double-click start-windows.bat. It installs anything new by itself.
- Few clips from the laugh detector: nobody gets excited on the video; use
  "With Claude chat" instead.
- The page is blank or stuck: refresh it.
Send a screenshot of any error and it can be fixed.

Only clip videos you own or have permission to use.
